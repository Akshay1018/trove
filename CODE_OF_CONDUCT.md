# Code of Conduct

Trove is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful community for everyone. This policy applies to project spaces and public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and inclusive, and use respectful language.
- Listen to differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community and show empathy toward others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberately disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer at ak715173@gmail.com. The maintainer will review reports and take any response considered appropriate to protect the community. Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as possible.
